Objective

›

This activity strengthened understanding of how machine learning models are trained by exploring supervised learning, unsupervised learning, reinforcement learning, algorithms, training processes, iteration, and the role of data through an interactive AI conversation.

02

Process

›

Worked with the Machine Learning Training Methods Coach in SchoolAI to explore seven questions related to model training. Rather than simply requesting answers, the exchange involved explaining each concept, responding to follow-up questions, and connecting the concepts to practical examples.

Value Proposition

›

This artifact demonstrates the ability to understand and communicate foundational machine learning concepts while using an AI assistant as a collaborative learning tool. It also demonstrates critical thinking by connecting theoretical concepts such as training, optimization, iteration, and data quality to practical machine learning scenarios.

“Data is not just something fed into a model; its quality, quantity, and representativeness directly affect what the model learns and how well it performs on new situations.”
